About the team

Raw Bacon Robotics is a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team at Iowa City High School in Iowa City, Iowa. Sizzling since our rookie year in 2014.

Mission

Build competitive robots and confident engineers. A hands-on path into STEM for City High students, shared with Iowa City and Johnson County.

Values

What we value

Student-led, staff-supported
Students make the design calls, write the code and drive. Adults keep it safe and keep it moving.
Gracious Professionalism
We compete hard and help the team across the field. Both are part of FIRST.
Iterate in public
We test early, share what broke and fix it where people can see.
Open doors
No experience required. Members learn CAD, Java, machining and presenting from people who learned them here.
History

Timeline

  1. 2014

    Rookie season. Raw Bacon Robotics starts competing in FIRST Tech Challenge.

  2. 2015–24

    A decade of seasons: new games, new robots, and students who went on to build things elsewhere.

  3. 2025

    DECODE season. Eight events, three first-place qualification finishes, an Innovate Award in second place, and a finish at the Iowa Championship, Black Division.

  4. 2026

    BIOBUZZ is under way, in the Zinnia league.
