About the team

Raw Bacon Robotics is a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team at Iowa City High School in Iowa City, Iowa. We have competed since our rookie season in 2014.

Our mission

Build competitive robots and confident engineers. We give students at City High a hands-on path into STEM, and we share that path with Iowa City and Johnson County.

What we stand for

Student-led, staff-supported
Students make the design, code, outreach and budget decisions. Mentors and school staff coach us along the way.
Gracious Professionalism
Helping other teams matters as much as the score.
Iterate in public
We document our designs in an engineering portfolio so others can follow our reasoning and learn from it.
Open doors
No experience is required. New members learn CAD, Java, machining and presentation skills on the team.

Team history

  1. 2014

    Our rookie season.

  2. 2015 to 2024

    A decade of seasons, qualifiers and judged awards, and of students moving on to engineering and computer science.

  3. 2025

    We competed at the Machu Picchu Qualifier and the Iowa Championship Black Division, and earned 2nd place for the Innovate Award. See the results.
