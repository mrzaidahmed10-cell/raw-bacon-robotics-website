The robot

Everything on it was designed, built and wired by students at Iowa City High School.

Drive

Mecanum wheels

Each wheel carries rollers set at 45 degrees. Run the wheels in different combinations and the robot drives forward, slides sideways or spins, without ever steering.

That freedom is why a robot like ours can line up a shot from any angle. Drag the wheel to turn it.

Illustration, not to scale.

Anatomy

What you are looking at

  • Number plateEvery FTC robot wears its team number. Ours is 8743.
  • Mecanum wheelAngled rollers let the robot move in any direction.
  • Perforated channelHoles along the whole length, so anything can bolt anywhere.
  • Motors and wiringRouted, zip-tied and fused by students.
Process

How a robot gets made

  • DesignOnshape CAD. Everyone can open the model and see why a part is shaped the way it is.
  • PrototypeRapid prototypes first. Drivetrain and intake are revised until they hold up.
  • ProgramJava on the REV Control Hub, with an odometry-based autonomous.
  • TunePID tuning and driver-practice telemetry, then back to the shop.
