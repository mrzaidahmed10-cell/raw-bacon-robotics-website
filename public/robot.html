The robot

Everything on it was designed, built and wired by students at Iowa City High School.

Anatomy of the robot

Motors and wiring

Routed, zip-tied and fused by students. Clean wiring is half of a reliable robot.

Perforated channel

Holes along the whole length, so parts can bolt on almost anywhere and weight stays low.

Number plate

Every FTC robot wears its team number. Ours is 8743, readable from across the field.

Mecanum wheel

Rollers set at 45 degrees let the robot drive forward, slide sideways or turn without steering.

Process

How a robot gets made

  • DesignOnshape CAD. Everyone can open the model and see why a part is shaped the way it is.
  • PrototypeRapid prototypes first. Drivetrain and intake are revised until they hold up.
  • ProgramJava on the REV Control Hub, with an odometry-based autonomous.
  • TunePID tuning and driver-practice telemetry, then back to the shop.
