How we work

Build

We design in Onshape, prototype fast and rebuild what does not hold up. Drivetrain and intake get revised until they do.

Program

Java on the REV Control Hub. Odometry-based autonomous, tuned PID, and telemetry we use in driver practice.

Reach out

Demos for Iowa City elementary and middle schools, mentoring for FTC and FLL teams, and STEM nights across Johnson County.

Built with help.

Sponsorship pays for parts, registration and travel. Mentors with engineering, software, machining or business backgrounds can make a difference with an hour a week.
