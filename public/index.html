How the team works

Build

We design in Onshape before we cut anything, prototype quickly, and revise the drivetrain and intake between competition weekends.

Programming

Java on the REV Control Hub. Our autonomous routines use odometry and tuned PID control, and telemetry from driver practice shows us what to fix next.

Outreach

We run demos for Iowa City elementary and middle schools, mentor newer FTC and FLL teams, and take part in STEM nights across Johnson County.

Sponsors

Sponsorship pays for parts, registration and travel, and it puts your name in front of our community all season. Sponsor slots for the coming season are open.
