How we work

Three jobs, one team.

Build
We design in Onshape, prototype quickly and rebuild whatever does not hold up. Drivetrain and intake get revised until they do.
Program
Java on the REV Control Hub. Odometry-based autonomous, tuned PID, and telemetry we use in driver practice.
Reach out
Demos for Iowa City elementary and middle schools, mentoring for FTC and FLL teams, and STEM nights across Johnson County.

Built with help.

Sponsorship pays for parts, registration and travel. A mentor with engineering, software, machining or business experience can change a season with an hour a week.
