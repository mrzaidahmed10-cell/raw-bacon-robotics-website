Reading the table

Qual rank
Where we finished after the qualification matches, before alliance selection.
Record
Wins, losses and ties in qualification matches.
RP
Average ranking points per qualification match.
OPR
Offensive Power Rating, an estimate of how many points a team adds to its alliance's score.
npOPR
OPR calculated without penalty points.
npAVG
Average alliance score per match, not counting penalty points.
