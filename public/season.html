Reading the numbers

OPR
Offensive power rating. An estimate of how many points a team adds to its alliance score.
npOPR
OPR with penalty points removed.
npAVG
Average alliance score without penalties.
RP
Average ranking points earned per qualification match.
Record
Qualification wins, losses and ties.
Source
